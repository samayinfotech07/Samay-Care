### Important Google SEO rule

Do **not** implement `<meta name="keywords">` as an SEO ranking mechanism. Google explicitly states that the `meta keywords` tag is ignored for indexing and ranking.

Use the keyword lists in this document as **content/search-intent targets**, not as a literal `meta keywords` tag.

Google guidance:
- Meta keywords are ignored: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/seo-starter-guide
- Supported meta tags: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/special-tags
- Title links: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/title-link
- Meta descriptions/snippets: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/snippet

# 2. Samay Care SEO Positioning

## Primary positioning

Samay Care should be discoverable around the problem:

> **Getting practical help through the hospital journey when a patient or family member cannot manage everything alone.**

The core service should be described as a **healthcare convenience / hospital companion / patient assistance service**, not as a nursing agency or generic home-care provider.

### Core service concepts

Use these concepts consistently:

- CareBuddy
- Healthcare convenience
- Hospital companion
- Patient companion
- Hospital visit assistance
- OPD assistance
- IPD / hospital stay assistance
- Hospital navigation
- Appointment assistance
- Queue assistance
- Documentation / paperwork assistance
- Diagnostic / pharmacy coordination
- Family updates
- Medical visit coordination
- Support for patients and attendants
- Assistance for families who cannot be physically present

### Positioning guardrails

Do NOT position Samay Care as:

- an eldercare-only company
- a caregiver marketplace
- a nursing agency
- a home nursing provider
- a medical emergency service
- a replacement for doctors or nurses
- a diagnostic laboratory
- a hospital
- a medical treatment provider

Use "non-clinical assistance", "coordination", "companionship", and "healthcare convenience" where appropriate.

# 27. Robots / Indexing

Do not block important SEO pages in `robots.txt`.

Do not use `noindex` on pages that are intended to rank.

For pages intentionally excluded from search, use the correct `noindex` implementation.

Google notes that `robots.txt` controls crawling, while `noindex` controls indexing. A URL that is disallowed from crawling may prevent Google from discovering the `noindex` instruction.

Source:
https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/robots-meta-tag
