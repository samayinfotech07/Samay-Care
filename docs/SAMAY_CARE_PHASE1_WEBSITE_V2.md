## 2. THE CORE STORY

A loved one needs healthcare.

The family may not be able to be physically present. They may be in another city, at work, travelling or abroad. Even when they are present, hospitals can be confusing and time-consuming.

The healthcare journey can involve:

**Appointment → Registration → Token → Queue → Navigation → Consultation → Diagnostics → Pharmacy → Admission → Reports → Follow-up**

Samay Care simplifies this journey by putting a trusted CareBuddy on the ground.

The CareBuddy can:
- accompany the patient
- stand in queues / obtain tokens
- navigate hospital processes
- coordinate appointments, diagnostics and pharmacy
- help the patient understand what is happening
- help with medical terminology/instructions within the non-clinical service boundary
- keep the family updated
- provide detailed journey updates
- coordinate attendant responsibilities

Clinical decisions remain with qualified healthcare professionals.

## 31. FAQ

### What is Samay Care?
Samay Care is a healthcare convenience platform that helps patients and families navigate the OPD and IPD journey with CareBuddy assistance.

### What is a CareBuddy?
A CareBuddy is a trained/verified, non-clinical human assistant who can accompany a patient, help navigate hospital processes and keep the family informed.

### Can a CareBuddy meet me at the hospital?
Yes. One service mode is **Meet at Hospital**, subject to launch availability.

### Can a CareBuddy accompany me from home?
Yes. **Accompany from Home** is part of the CareBuddy service model, subject to launch availability.

### Is a CareBuddy a doctor or nurse?
No. CareBuddy provides non-clinical assistance. Clinical decisions remain with qualified healthcare professionals.

### Can my family receive updates?
Samay Care is designed to help families stay informed about the healthcare journey, subject to the service and technology available at launch.

### Is Samay Care available in my city?
Samay Care is launching city by city. Visitors can submit their interest to help prioritize launches.
