---

# 1. BRAND FOUNDATION

## 1.1 What is Samay Care?

Samay Care is a healthcare convenience platform designed to reduce the non-clinical hassle patients and families face around healthcare.

The first service is **CareBuddy**.

A CareBuddy is the trained human who assists the customer.

The simplest analogy:

> Ride-hailing apps connect customers with drivers.  
> Food delivery platforms connect customers with riders.  
> **Samay Care connects customers with CareBuddies.**

The CareBuddy is the human layer of Samay Care.

---

# 2. WHY THE NAME "SAMAY CARE"

## Samay

"Samay" means **time**.

Healthcare is time-sensitive.

Appointments, diagnostics, admissions, reports, medicines and follow-ups often need to happen at the right time.

Patients and families should not have to lose that valuable time dealing with unnecessary operational hassle.

## Care

Care needs no definition.

It communicates:

- empathy
- support
- trust
- human connection
- responsibility

Together:

> **Samay Care = Time + Care**

Brand thought:

> **Time. Care. Always.**

Official tagline:

> **Making Healthcare Convenient.**

# 5. CAREBUDDY SERVICE MODEL

The customer can book a CareBuddy through the Samay Care app.

Two core booking modes:

## Meet at Hospital

The customer reaches the hospital independently.

The CareBuddy meets the customer there and assists with the non-clinical hospital journey.

## Accompany from Home

The CareBuddy comes to the customer's home and accompanies the customer to the hospital.

The CareBuddy then assists through the hospital journey.

This distinction should appear consistently across:

- website
- app
- marketing
- pitch deck
- advertisements
- onboarding
- sales material

# 12. COLOR RATIO

Recommended visual ratio:

```text
White / Light Surface     65–75%
Teal / Healthcare Green  15–20%
Navy                     8–12%
Other Accent Colors      Minimal
```

Teal should be recognizable without overwhelming the page.

Avoid turning the entire website green.

---

# 13. COLOR RULES

Do:

- Use teal to guide attention.
- Use navy for authority.
- Use white for breathing room.
- Use mint backgrounds to signal healthcare.
- Keep contrast accessible.

Do not:

- Use multiple competing brand colors.
- Use bright red as a normal CTA.
- Use purple as a primary brand color.
- Use orange as a dominant accent.
- Use gradients that overpower photography.

# 50. BRAND DO / DON'T

## DO

- Be warm.
- Be professional.
- Be clear.
- Show real people.
- Show technology as an enabler.
- Explain CareBuddy simply.
- Use teal consistently.
- Respect patients.
- Maintain clinical boundaries.

## DON'T

- Make medical claims.
- Make fake scale claims.
- Use fake testimonials.
- Use unauthorized hospital logos.
- Make CareBuddy look like a doctor.
- Overuse healthcare clichés.
- Make the site look like a hospital.
- Make the brand childish.
- Overuse gradients.
- Overpromise launch availability.
